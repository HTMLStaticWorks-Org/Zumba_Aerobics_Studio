Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before attending your first studio class.

Wear breathable, moisture-wicking athletic attire and supportive sneakers with smooth or pivot-point soles. Avoid running shoes with aggressive outdoor tread which can grip too hard during rapid turns.
No need to carry equipment! We provide professional shock-absorbing aerobic step platforms with adjustable risers, sanitized 10mm mats, and resistance bands free of charge.
There are no wrong moves at Pulse&Motion—only accidental solos! The main goal is to keep your heart pumping, smile, and have fun. Our certified instructors demonstrate beginner variations for every single track.
Please arrive 10–15 minutes early so we can show you to your locker, guide you through the studio amenities, and introduce you to your coach.
